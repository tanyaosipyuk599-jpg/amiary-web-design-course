The problem

Meaningful memories rarely stay in one place

They live in old messages, half-finished conversations, photos on different phones and stories that only get told once. An ordinary gift can't hold any of that.

Scattered

Photos, voice notes and stories stay scattered across people, devices and years.

Forgotten

The smallest, most revealing stories fade when no one records them.

One-sided

One person remembers only one version of a life.

What is AMIARY

One private portrait, made of many memories

An AMIARY brings together written memories, photos and personal messages about one specific person, gathered from the people who know them.

The process

From first thought to finished portrait

A private process with four clear stages, however many people take part.

1

Choose the recipient

Start an AMIARY for the person you want to honour, and pick the occasion behind it.

2

Add or collect memories

Write your own memories, or privately invite others to share theirs, depending on the edition.

3

Shape the private portrait

Review what's been gathered and arrange it into one cohesive, personal piece.

4

Reveal the AMIARY

Share the finished portrait with the recipient, at the moment that feels right.

Two ways to create one

Choose how the AMIARY comes together

Both editions produce the same kind of private memory portrait. The difference is who contributes to it.

Made alone

AMIARY One

One organiser creates the entire memory portrait by themselves — writing the stories, choosing the photos and shaping the whole piece from start to finish.

  • Best for a focused, personal gift from one person
  • The organiser has full control over every part
  • No need to involve or wait on other contributors
Made together

AMIARY Together

The organiser privately invites friends and family to add their own memories, photos and messages, then shapes everything into one finished portrait.

  • Best when many people share a connection to the recipient
  • Invitations are private and controlled by the organiser
  • The organiser reviews and shapes what's collected

Privacy

Private by design, from the first invitation

An AMIARY is made for one person, not for the internet. Every part of it stays under the organiser's control.

Private by design

The memory portrait is never public and can't be browsed or discovered by anyone outside it.

Organiser-controlled invitations

Only people the organiser personally invites can view or contribute to an AMIARY Together.

Not the recipient's to find

The recipient's story stays private until the organiser chooses to reveal it.
